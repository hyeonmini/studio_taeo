Frequently Asked Questions

How do I share a household with my partner?
Create a household in the app, then share the invite code with your partner. They can join by tapping "Join with Invite Code" on the start screen.
Can I use WeSpend without creating an account?
On Android, tap "Guest" to create an anonymous account. Keep the app installed while using a guest account because it has no separate login credential.
How do I change the currency?
Your household's default currency is set when creating the household. To record an expense in another currency, tap the currency icon on Android or long-press it on iOS.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Delete Account. If you no longer have access to the app, use our account deletion request page.
Is my data secure?
WeSpend uses TLS encryption and access-controlled Firebase services. Optional AI features send only the content needed to process your request to Anthropic. See the Privacy Policy for all processors and details.
